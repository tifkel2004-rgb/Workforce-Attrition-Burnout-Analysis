# Data-Driven Workforce Attrition Risk & Organizational Health Dashboard
👉 [**Click here to interact with the live Tableau Dashboard**](https://public.tableau.com/authoring/WorkforceAttritionandBurnoutRiskAnalysis/Sheet1/Overtime%20Burnout%20%26%20Employee%20Attrition%20Trends#1)


## 📌 Project Executive Summary
Managing retention and preventing employee burnout is one of the most critical challenges facing complex institutional landscapes. This analytics project targets the underlying structural, economic, and behavioral drivers that influence employee attrition. 

By modeling a multi-variable workforce dataset containing 1,470 personnel records across 35 variables, this interactive business intelligence tool equips HR leadership and institutional decision-makers with the descriptive insights needed to proactively mitigate turnover risks, optimize compensation strategies, and improve organizational satisfaction.

## 🛠️ Core Competencies & Tools Demonstrated
* **Data Visualization & BI:** Tableau Desktop (Calculated Fields, Dynamic Filters, Interactive Parameters).
* **Data Modeling:** Structural integration of discrete personnel logs (demographics, job classifications, performance metrics).
* **Strategic Workforce Metrics:** Attrition Rate Tracking, Compensation Equity Analysis, Work-Life Balance Correlations.

---

## 📈 Key Dashboard Metrics & Strategic Insights

### 1. The Burnout Indicator: Overtime vs. Retention
* **Insight:** Personnel working overtime exhibit a significantly higher turnover rate compared to standard-hour employees. This highlights operational burnout as a key predictive indicator for voluntary exits.
* **Strategic Recommendation:** Implement departmental thresholds on continuous overtime and establish formal wellness/workload checkpoints for high-risk teams.

### 2. Economic Triggers: Compensation Distribution
* **Insight:** Attrition spikes heavily among employees in lower monthly income tiers who also report stagnant promotion histories, showing that compensation equity is directly linked to mid-career retention.
* **Strategic Recommendation:** Conduct targeted equity reviews for employees who have hit career ceilings within their current job classifications.

### 3. Cultural Factors: Managerial and Work Environment Scores
* **Insight:** Lower scores in "Relationship with Manager" and "Work Environment Satisfaction" strongly correlate with early-stage voluntary attrition (employees with less than 2 years of tenure).
* **Strategic Recommendation:** Enhance early-stage mentorship programs and introduce anonymous quarterly pulse surveys to gauge department culture before departures occur.
